LG Energy Solution opened its Michigan battery cell factory after converting part of the facility to serve fast-growing grid energy-storage demand.

Company leaders said the plant will strengthen domestic supply chains, support skilled manufacturing jobs, and supply lithium iron phosphate cells to storage projects across North America.

The factory is expected to reach full production next year as utilities and developers add batteries to meet rising electricity demand and balance renewable generation.
